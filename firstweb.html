One of the most challenging parts of my internship was dealing with incomplete and inconsistent data. For example, during my project at Lord Ai, when I was scraping risk analytics data, I noticed that many fields were missing or in the wrong format. Initially, this made the analysis less reliable.

To overcome this, I researched different data cleaning techniques, used Python (Pandas and NumPy) to handle missing values, and standardized the formats before running EDA. It took extra time, but the quality of insights improved significantly.
